CampusCart 🛒

Your Campus, Your Marketplace.

CampusCart is a student-focused marketplace that connects student sellers and buyers within their campus community.

🚀 Our Mission

To make buying and selling within college campuses simple, convenient, and accessible.

✨ Planned Features

- 🛍️ Browse products listed by students
- 🏪 Create seller listings
- 🔎 Search for products
- 📦 Submit and manage order requests
- 🎓 Discover a campus-focused marketplace

🛠️ Project Status

Currently in development. We are building our first Minimum Viable Product (MVP).

🎯 Our Goal

To create a functional marketplace that students can test and use.
